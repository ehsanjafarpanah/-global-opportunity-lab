# Commodities and Metals Module

Identify the exposure precisely: spot benchmark, physical market, ETF or specific futures contract/month. Review inventories, production, demand, seasonality, curve/term structure, roll yield, supply disruptions, USD and real rates where relevant. Starting sources: EIA for US energy data, USDA for agricultural releases, CFTC COT for delayed positioning, exchange contract specifications and ETF issuer reports.

Do not equate futures volume with spot volume or ignore expiry, delivery and roll mechanics.
