## 3. Three focused research tracks

### A. BTC-USD — bearish structure, but wait for price confirmation
- **Evidence:** Nov 24 close/quotes clustered around $59.6k–$59.9k in public chart summaries. One chart source identified nearby resistance around $60,835 and support around $57,748; another reported $58k–$58.5k as a key support zone and $64,600 as a larger recovery trigger. ETF flows were negative over the broader recent window.
- **Bullish confirmation:** Reclaim and hold above ~$60.8k, followed by stronger confirmation above ~$64.6k; ETF flows stabilize/turn positive and spot volume supports the move.
- **Bearish continuation confirmation:** Daily close below ~$57.7k–$58k with expanding spot volume and no rapid reclaim.
- **Invalidation:** For a bearish continuation thesis, sustained reclaim of ~$64.6k invalidates the immediate breakdown thesis. For a recovery thesis, a decisive loss of ~$57.7k invalidates the base/reclaim idea.
- **Decision:** Watch only. Oversold readings alone are not an entry signal.

### B. DELL — strong fundamental catalyst, elevated gap risk
- **Evidence:** Reuters reported record quarterly revenue, higher annual guidance, $60.2B of new AI-server orders and $132.3B backlog. Reuters reported roughly +5% after-hours in its report; market summaries show differing price reactions, so the exact opening gap must be checked against the exchange feed.
- **Bullish confirmation:** Hold the post-earnings gap, build a higher low, and break the first post-gap range high on above-average volume.
- **Bearish/invalidation:** Gap failure and a close back below the post-earnings support / prior range would negate the continuation thesis.
- **Decision:** Research priority, not an entry. Do not chase the first gap candle; confirm price/volume and updated guidance call details.

### C. Copper — potential contrarian watch, not yet a reversal
- **Evidence:** ING described copper giving back its Nvidia-led rebound on Nov 24 despite physical tightness; Saxo's Nov 23 COT note described speculative copper longs at a 14-month low. This can create a watchable asymmetry, but weak macro/risk appetite may prolong the decline.
- **Bullish confirmation:** Price forms a higher low, reclaims a short-term breakdown level, and exchange inventory/physical premium data corroborate tightening.
- **Invalidation:** Fresh low with accelerating selling and no improvement in physical indicators.
- **Decision:** Watch for base formation only. No long before technical confirmation.

## Copper — downgrade the bullish contrarian thesis
- LME copper stocks increased from 274,000 tonnes on Oct 30 to 330,975 tonnes on Nov 23 (about +20.8%). LME 3-month copper fell from $14,250/t to $12,170/t over the same dates.
- This combination does not support the earlier shorthand that physical tightness is already corroborating a bullish reversal. Warehouse stocks are only one part of the physical balance (they do not include all off-warrant, bonded or exchange inventories), but the observed build is a material caution.
- **Revised state:** Monitor / no long thesis until inventories, spreads and physical premiums provide corroboration and price establishes a higher low.
- **Revised provisional score:** 55/100 (from 65). This is a research-priority downgrade, not a probability.
