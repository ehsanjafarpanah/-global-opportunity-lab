# Scoring Model

Rate each dimension from 0 to 5. Weighted points = weight × rating / 5.

| Dimension | Weight | Assessment |
|---|---:|---|
| Catalyst / driver strength | 20 | Verifiability, materiality, timing, impact |
| Technical structure / momentum | 20 | Trend, levels, setup, relative strength, confirmation |
| Capital flows / activity | 15 | Relevant spot/ETF/fund flows, volume, positioning, liquidity |
| Fundamentals / valuation | 15 | Operating, valuation, supply or macro evidence |
| Independent sentiment / attention | 10 | Breadth, quality, change and corroboration |
| Macro / regime fit | 10 | Alignment or resilience under prevailing regime |
| Risk / supply / positioning | 10 | Liquidity, dilution/unlocks, concentration, crowding, event risk |

Score = sum(weight × rating / 5).

## Interpretation
- 85–100: exceptional research priority; hard gates still apply.
- 75–84: strong watchlist candidate.
- 65–74: watch / await better evidence.
- 50–64: monitor only.
- Below 50: normally reject.

## Hard gates (override score)
Not Entry Candidate if instrument/venue/currency is ambiguous; liquidity or spread is unsuitable; core catalyst is unverified or data stale; invalidation cannot be defined; estimated R/R is below default 2:1 before costs; price is materially extended; near-term event creates unquantifiable gap/binary risk; or material evidence contradictions remain unresolved.

## Market-specific interpretation
- Crypto: separate spot/derivatives; token value capture, circulating supply, unlocks, protocol usage/fees, venue concentration and liquidity. TVL alone is not proof of token value.
- Equities: filings, earnings/revisions, guidance, balance sheet, valuation, float, dilution, corporate actions and earnings gap risk.
- Commodities: contract/benchmark, inventories, curve, seasonality, supply, USD and real rates as relevant.
- FX: relative policy, rate differentials, inflation/growth surprises, positioning and technical structure; no consolidated spot volume.
- ETFs/indices: flows, breadth, constituents, concentration, sector rotation, tracking/liquidity and rebalance.

Mark genuinely inapplicable metrics N/A. Use documented market-specific profiles; never assign zero just because data is unavailable. The model ranks research candidates; it is not a probability of profit.
