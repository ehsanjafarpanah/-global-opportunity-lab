# Risk and Trade Qualification Framework

## Operating stance
Medium risk tolerance; wait for confirmation; do not chase. Research score does not determine position size. No trade is mandatory.

## Before entry
Define instrument, venue, order type, quote currency and execution constraints; entry trigger/zone; thesis/structure-based invalidation; spread, fees, slippage, funding/borrow and FX costs; reward/risk after costs; event calendar, gaps, expiry and liquidity; portfolio correlation and aggregate exposure.

## Risk controls
Position size must be calculated from a user-defined account risk budget and stop distance. Do not assume account size or prescribe a fixed percentage without context. For derivatives, document leverage, liquidation distance, funding, margin type and gap risk. If these cannot be quantified, research-only.

## Wait-style entry protocol
1. Price reaches planned area.
2. Required confirmation appears (close/reclaim, successful retest, flow or catalyst confirmation).
3. Thesis remains valid and R/R remains acceptable after costs.
4. No disqualifying event or data-quality issue has emerged.
5. Only then may status become Entry Candidate.

Do not average down solely to reduce cost basis. Do not move invalidation farther away after entry without documenting a thesis revision.

## Invalidation and exit
Record price-based and thesis-based invalidation separately. Define target logic and time stop before entry. If catalyst fails, evidence reverses or invalidation is hit, update status promptly while preserving the original record.

## Portfolio checks
Avoid stacking correlated exposures under different tickers. Consider market beta, currency, sector, venue/counterparty and simultaneous event risks. If portfolio context is unknown, report trade-level risk only and state the limitation.

Educational research documentation; not personalized financial advice.
